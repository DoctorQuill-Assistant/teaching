## Coaching notes
- The reduction is based on the 31 July early stop: very tired legs plus pain around/between the knee. Do not compensate for the shortened Friday or earlier shorter long run.
- Use a simple pain rule: no run if pain is present while walking, increases while running, changes gait, is sharp, or is worse later that day/next morning. Stop a test run at once if any of these occur.
- Seek prompt medical assessment for swelling, locking, giving way/instability, inability to bear weight, a hot/red joint, or significant worsening; otherwise seek a clinician/physio if symptoms do not settle over several days.
- Keep all running effort-first: full-sentence conversational effort, flat route, and no pace goal. Heat makes a return test less useful; run early or skip it in hot conditions.
- Please log whether the pain is on one side or central, its severity (0–10), and how it responds during/after walking or running. That evidence determines the next progression.
